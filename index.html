How the PLINKO GAME board works

The board is shaped like a triangle of pegs. A Plinko ball starts at the top, then moves left or right each time it hits a peg until it reaches the bottom row. That repeated left-or-right motion is what creates the random-looking payout pattern that players see in Plinko gambling.

Because most paths drift toward the middle, centre pockets are hit more often than edge pockets. This is why the middle payouts are usually smaller, while the outer pockets may pay much more. In simple terms, PLINKO GAME turns probability into a visible board layout that is easy to read even for first-time players.
